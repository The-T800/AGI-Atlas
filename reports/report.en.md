## Method

The work proxy multiplies relative benchmark scores by coverage factors: direct 1.0, partial 0.5; missing evidence contributes zero. The subject axis shows task links and original benchmark results, with unknowns preserved. It does not compute subject mastery or AGI completion.

**Limitations**: Mappings are editorial and not independently reviewed; some scores are vendor-reported. Based on the US occupational system; occupations are equally weighted, not by employment. Research topics are not an exhaustive capability inventory. Mappings describe task relevance, not subject mastery. Broad mappings never populate child topics. Unmeasured topics are unknown; benchmark totals are not subject scores.
